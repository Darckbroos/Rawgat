OUR STORY

A climber's company, built from home.

I'm Bastián, just a climber from Chile. Growing up, I watched, listened to and lived stories alongside adventurers: people who had lived life on the edge, with a strange sense of existence that's often hard to understand. Somewhere along the way I realised I wanted to live, create and be part of the moments, in sport and in life, that people go through every day. That vision slowly became an idea, and that's how RAWGAT was born: an idea that brings together everything I care about. Climbing, mountain adventures and community in sport.

It all starts in a small workshop at home in Spain, still taking shape. That's where I design and 3D print every piece. Then I train with them, and so do climber friends I meet along the way. What they feel in training goes straight into the next version.

RAWGAT is for climbers who train on purpose: people who plan their sessions, follow their progress and want to keep climbing for years. No miracle promises. Tools that do one job well, with clear information on how to use them.

The name.

Raw is me: direct, sometimes blunt, no filter. That's how this brand talks. Gat is short for gato, cat in Spanish, which is what my friends call me. Put them together and you get the cat on the mountain in our logo.

What's next.

RAWGAT products are designed to grow, and future versions may bring technology into your training. That's a roadmap, not a feature. When it exists, you'll see it here. Until then, we only sell what's ready.
