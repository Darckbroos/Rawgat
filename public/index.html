Our story

Born on the rock, not in the office

RAWGAT was born from a real need, not a trend: finding a mountain brand that stands behind what it sells. Tired of gear that fell apart after one season and ended up in the trash, we bet on recycling, on repairing instead of replacing, and said no to fast fashion. Every piece is tested on the wall, on boulders and on real rock before it ever goes on sale.

That's the same standard we demand of the people we dress: those who don't compromise on their gear because their safety depends on it — elite, professional, high-risk. The name comes from that idea: raw (authentic, unrefined) and gat, the Raw Cat that inspires our logo — half mountain, half feline, a balance between instinct and discipline.
