The RAWGAT fit isn't a street fit

Our garments are patterned to move on the wall, not on the street: an articulated cut that follows the knee through a high-step, a waistband that doesn't slip when you reach up for a jug. If you're used to streetwear sizing, it's normal for your RAWGAT size to feel different — snugger exactly where it should be, with more freedom where it matters.

How to choose it

  • Each product shows which sizes are available and which are sold out — you can only select a size with real stock.
  • If you're between two sizes, for pants and technical tees it's usually better to size up: it gives more freedom of movement while climbing without losing the cut.
  • For body-fitted pieces (technical tees), stick to your usual size unless you prefer a looser fit.

How to measure yourself at home

  • Chest/bust: measuring tape around the widest part of your chest, parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: at the narrowest point of your torso, without pulling tight.
  • Hip: around the widest part of your hips/glutes.

Still unsure?

Write to us before buying — it's easier to sort out beforehand than after a return. And if the size still isn't right, you have 30 days to return it at no cost.
