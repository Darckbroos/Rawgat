RAWGAT Commitment

Your gear, genuinely backed

We don't sell disposable gear. Every RAWGAT piece is tested on the wall, on boulders and on real rock before it goes on sale, and we back it with a real warranty — no fine print. Here's what it covers and how to claim it.

5 years warranty on seams
30 days on-the-rock trial
30 days for returns

What the seam warranty covers

  • Manufacturing defects in seams during the first 5 years from purchase.
  • Repair or replacement of the garment, depending on what the defect calls for.
  • Applies to the original buyer, with the order linked to your RAWGAT account.

What it doesn't cover

  • Normal wear and tear from use (rock abrasion, fading, pilling).
  • Damage from misuse, incorrect washing, or unauthorized modifications to the garment.
  • Garments bought second-hand or outside rawgat.com.

30-day on-the-rock trial

In addition to the seam warranty, you have 30 days from receiving your order to try the garment in real conditions — wall, boulder, route — and decide if it's for you. If it doesn't work out, our returns policy applies: unused, with tags, and in its original packaging.

How to claim your warranty

  1. Write to us at [contact email] with your order number.
  2. Attach 2-3 clear photos of the seam defect.
  3. We'll confirm within [response time, e.g. 3 business days] whether repair or replacement applies, and the next shipping steps.

We repair, not replace

RAWGAT was founded on a bet for recycling and repairing instead of replacing — we said no to fast fashion. That's why, whenever the defect allows it, we prioritize repairing your garment over replacing it: it's the approach most consistent with why this brand exists.
