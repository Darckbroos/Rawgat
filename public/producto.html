Before you train

  • Check the tool and its attachment before every session. Stop if you see cracks, deformation or movement in the mount.
  • Build load gradually and stop if you feel pain.
  • If you're training after an injury, talk to a physiotherapist or doctor first. RAWGAT tools support training; they don't diagnose, treat or prevent injuries.
